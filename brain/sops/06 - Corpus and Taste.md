# 06 Corpus and Taste
> Up: [[00 - Index|Agent SOPs]]

**The principle.** Nothing the human says that reveals who they are, how they think, what they want or how they speak should have to be reverse-engineered from a dead transcript. Their words are calibration data. They compound.

**Layer 1, the Corpus.** `Personal/Corpus.md`, plus one per pillar for domain-specific rulings (pointed to from the master, never duplicated). Verbatim: preserve phrasing, rhythm, word choice; light spelling cleanup only; if a line is important and messy, keep the mess. Dated, appended under a date header with a one-line context tag. Append only; never edit or delete a past entry.

**What goes in.** Rulings in their own framing. Philosophy and how they see the work. Preferences and corrections about how work should be done (these also become memory). Self-observations, especially a named limit, fear or pattern. Distinctive language.

**What does not.** Task mechanics and status (SOP 05). Third-party content they pasted (SOP 09). Anything they ask not to record.

**Layer 2, the Taste Record.** Every design decision, rejection and swap is a rule about taste, not a note about one artifact. Capture the reason under the rejection, and what it went FROM and TO, because the delta is the taste. Record the named tells (what they call fake, AI-made, templated); those are the hardest to re-derive. Record ratified numbers as law. Tag each rule by how much variance the artifact class allows: a report or a model should regenerate identically from a template, a website is roughly 60 percent fixed and the rest is supposed to differ, creative repeats skeletons and varies content.

**The standard.** If the human gives the same correction twice, the first capture failed. Treat the second as a defect in this process, not as new information.
